Image Classifier and Search

Two demos that run entirely in your browser: classify an image with a CNN trained from scratch, or search photos with words or images using CLIP.

A small convolutional network trained in PyTorch on CIFAR-10, running in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web.

The model was trained on 32×32 CIFAR-10 images of 10 classes (airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck), so it works best on small, centered pictures of those classes. High-resolution photos are shrunk to 32×32 and may be misclassified.

Search 5,000 photos from COCO with words or with an image of your own. CLIP turns both texts and images into vectors in the same space, so the closest vectors are the best matches. Click any result to see similar photos.

The model is CLIP ViT-B/32, running in your browser with Transformers.js. The first search downloads it (about 65 MB, plus 90 MB for image uploads); later visits use the browser cache. Photos: COCO 2017 validation set.
