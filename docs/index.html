The model was trained on 32×32 CIFAR-10 images of 10 classes (airplane, automobile, bird, cat, deer, dog, frog, horse, ship, truck), so it works best on small, centered pictures of those classes. High-resolution photos are shrunk to 32×32 and may be misclassified.
